# sched2-us-football-basketball (fetched 2026-10-19)
Scope: US football and basketball catalog teams (football: NFL, CFL, college FBS/FCS conferences, UFL, IFL, AF1; basketball: NBA, WNBA, G League, men's and women's college conferences, history/* teams). Round 1 holds 2011-2026; this folder adds 2006-2010 and the leagues round 1 never had.
Audit (script in scratchpad, match by externalIds.espn + parent league file, abbreviation for teams.json rows, catalog id for IFL/AF1):
 teams in scope 1154 (football 345, basketball 809). Teams with ANY game data: before 1121 (football 312, basketball 809), after 1137 (football 328). Teams with pre-2011 data: before 0, after 1050 (football 299, basketball 751).
Sources: ESPN site API, one day per request (groups=80/81 FBS/FCS, 50 college basketball; limit 300/1000); Wikipedia/Wikidata official APIs (1 req/s, descriptive User-Agent, source "wikipedia"). Raw responses cached outside the repo.
Files (season = start year; one file per season, largest ~700 KB, none split): 47 files, 74,327 games, validator 0 errors.
 football-nfl 2006-10 1,335 | football-college-football (FBS, group 80) 4,713 | football-college-football-fcs (group 81) 4,197 (FBS-vs-FCS games sit in both, as in round 1)
 football-ufl 2024-26 121 (ESPN; no UFL in round 1) | football-cfl 2006-10 385 (Wikipedia team season pages) | football-ifl 2021, 2023, 2024 317 (Wikipedia) | football-af1 2025 45 (Wikipedia)
 basketball-nba 2006-10 6,566 | basketball-wnba 2006-10 1,221 | basketball-mens-college-basketball 28,425 | basketball-womens-college-basketball 27,002 (2006-10)
Included: regular season, playoffs/bowls/NCAA and conference tournaments, finals. Excluded: preseason (ESPN season.type 1), All-Star, Pro Bowl (NFL 31/32 AFC/NFC, WNBA East/West), TBD placeholders.
CFL: ESPN has no CFL before 2021, so each of the 8 clubs' "<year> <club> season" pages were merged (both clubs' pages must agree on date; date taken from the home club's page). 77 games per season (72 + 5 playoff). Kickoff times are not on the pages: 19:00 local, note "Kickoff time approximate".
 Home stadium comes from the page's venue column, else the infobox stadium (note "Venue inferred from home stadium"); Grey Cup venues from the Grey Cup articles. 2007 Grey Cup is missing from every team page and was taken from the 95th Grey Cup article. 6 games (5 in 2006, West Final 2009) have null scores because the two pages disagree ("Score uncertain").
IFL: Wikipedia has game-level schedules only for 2021, 2023, 2024 (2023 playoffs only as a bracket without venues: left out; 2021 cancelled games struck through on the page: left out). 2024 National Championship from the game box. AF1: 2025 regular season only (playoff dates not on the page).
 team ids in IFL/AF1 files are catalog ids (ifl-gb, af1-alb ...) or wp-<slug> for clubs not in the catalog; team-names.json maps id to name. Duke City Gladiators is NOT joined to ifl-nm (New Mexico Chupacabras): same market, franchise link unconfirmed.
Spot checks: NFL 16 games per team and 268 per season; NBA 82 per team for non-playoff teams (1,309-1,316 games); WNBA 34 for non-playoff teams; FBS teams 10-15, FCS 5-15; college basketball D1 teams 25-41 (non-D1 opponents kept with 1-3 games like round 1). ESPN FCS 2006 is thin (722 games vs 875 in 2010).
Venue gaps and what was inferred (never invented): ESPN lacks venues for many early games. A missing venue on a non-neutral game was filled with the home team's dominant venue that season (>=70%, >=2 games), else with the same venue seen on BOTH neighbouring seasons, else (one side only) a later venue that is in the catalog with opened <= 2005. Every such game says "Venue inferred from home stadium". Women's basketball (ESPN has no venues before 2011): used the men's team's arena that season only when men and women shared the arena in 2011-12; note "Venue inferred from men's team home arena" (16,685 games). Left null: women's 10,078, men's 1,933, WNBA 679 (no neighbour evidence), FCS 692, NFL 32 (2006-07 Colts: RCA Dome not provable), FBS 214, IFL 1.
 One bad ESPN venue removed (men's 2006: "Charlotte Coliseum" for a Charleston Southern game titled North Charleston Coliseum). WNBA franchise ids follow ESPN's current franchise (Detroit Shock appears as TUL).
Venues: venues-seen.json 1,240 records (1,129 matched to the app catalog by name+city, alias or <=150 m); missing-venues.json 82 physical venues, validator 0 errors, from round 1 missing files where the venue was already researched, otherwise Wikidata P625 cross-checked with Nominatim. Approximate: Schwartz Athletic Center (LIU campus), Brian Kelly '83 Stadium (Assumption campus), Allgood-Bailey Stadium and a few small IFL/AF1 arenas (OSM only). Demolished and kept separate: Giants Stadium, Texas Stadium, RCA Dome.
Unlockable:
 - NBA G League / D-League 2006-2017 (29 catalog teams have 2018+ only): ESPN returns 0 events, stats.gleague.nba.com and cdn.nba.com blocked; allowed source needed (NBA Stats API terms, or a licensed feed).
 - Indoor Football League 2009-2020, 2022, 2025-2026 and clubs without Wikipedia schedules (Arkansas Diamonds, Athens Crush, Fishers Freight, Orlando Pirates, Savannah Leprechauns, Texas Draft, San Antonio pre-2024): goifl.com publishes schedules, reuse terms not cleared; Wikipedia season pages lack game lists.
 - Arena Football One 2026 and playoff dates 2025 (ATX Bulls, Beaumont Renegades, Kentucky Barrels, Michigan Arsenal, Minnesota Monsters, San Diego Stealth): theaf1.com schedule pages, terms not cleared.
 - CFL 2020 cancelled (no data); 29 football teams have data only from 2011 or later (UFL 6, IFL/AF1 newcomers, college programs that were Division II/NAIA in 2006-2010 and so are not in ESPN groups 80/81).
Uncertain: CFL scores come from Wikipedia and are unverified beyond cross-checking the two clubs' pages; college football/basketball statuses "O" (1 game) are ESPN in-progress leftovers.

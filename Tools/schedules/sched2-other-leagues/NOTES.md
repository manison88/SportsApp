# sched2-other-leagues (fetched 2026-10-19)
Scope: every catalog team no other round-2 scope owns. Audit over all 4,589 catalog teams (teams.json, teams-more.json, national-teams.json) against every Tools/schedules/sched*/ folder (round 1, sched2-*, sched3-espn). After subtracting baseball, hockey, US football/basketball, European soccer, soccer outside Europe, national teams and European basketball/handball, one league is left: the Australian NBL (basketball/aus.nbl, 10 teams: nbl-mel/per/syd/adl/bne/ill/nzb/cns/sem/tas). Volleyball/rugby do not exist in the catalog; motorsport (F1, 11 teams) is handled elsewhere.
Teams in scope 10: with data before 0, after 10. (Whole catalog by the best-effort join in COVERAGE.md: 2,560 teams with round-1 data, 3,806 with data in any folder today.)

## Source (allowed: Wikipedia via its official endpoints, descriptive User-Agent, source "wikipedia")
ESPN has no NBL feed ("no usable feed" in sched3-espn/FETCH-LOG.txt); NBL's own site and FIBA are not allowed. Used the Wikipedia season pages "<y>-<y+1> NBL season", the "NBL regular season" sub-articles for 2018-19 to 2021-22 and the "<y> NBL Finals" pages for 2014 on. Games (date, local time, teams, score, venue) were read from the pages' game boxes (data-mw of the basketball-box templates, or the box/table HTML for older seasons). Pages were fetched through Wikipedia's page HTML / REST / parse API with 1.5-3 s spacing and backoff on 429 (the shared egress IP was heavily throttled); raw pages stay in the scratchpad.
Files: basketball-aus.nbl/2006.json .. 2026.json (21 files, 3,070 games, 495 KB, validator 0 errors). Season = start year (2006 = 2006-07). 2026 = 2026-27: 36 final, 126 scheduled (status S).
Included: regular season, finals (elimination/semi-finals/grand final series, 2022 on play-in tournament), the 2025-26 Ignite Cup final. Excluded: pre-season, NBL Blitz, All-Star games, and games with a team that is not an NBL club.
Spot check, regular-season games per team: 28 in 2009-2018 and 2021-2023, 30 in 2007-08, 33 in 2006-07 (12 clubs), 36 in 2020-21 (9 clubs), 29 in 2024-25, 33 in 2025-26. 2026-27 is only partly published (31-33 each).

## Teams / ids
Catalog ids for the 10 NBL clubs (join by id; team-names.json lists every id). Clubs not in the catalog get wp-<slug>: Townsville Crocodiles, Gold Coast Blaze, South Dragons, Singapore Slingers, West Sydney Razorbacks, Sydney Spirit.
Franchise links I made (judgement): Melbourne Tigers = Melbourne United; Wollongong Hawks = Illawarra Hawks (the Hawks are "The Hawks" in 2020-21); Brisbane Bullets (1979-2008 and from 2016) = nbl-bne; Sydney Kings (folded 2008, back 2010) = nbl-syd. South Dragons are NOT linked to South East Melbourne Phoenix (separate franchises).

## Times, venues, corrections (read before use)
- 1,552 games (mostly 2006-2013, some later) have no tip-off time on the page: startUTC uses 19:00 local and the note says "Tip-off time not published (19:00 local assumed)". Local times elsewhere are converted with the venue's IANA zone.
- Venue null: 2 games 2006 (Bridgecoast Stadium, place not identifiable), 1 game 2007 (page lists the team name as venue), 46 games of 2026-27 (page gives only a city; note says so). For another 116 2026-27 games whose city equals the home club's city, the venue is the club's home arena of 2025-26 (>= 70%, >= 5 games) and the note says "Venue inferred from home arena".
- 153 games carry "Neutral site": played in a city other than the home club's (NZ Breakers in Christchurch, regional rounds, 2020-21 New Zealand hub). Renamed arenas keep one id (Vodafone/Hisense/Melbourne Arena = John Cain Arena; Vector = Spark Arena; Wollongong EC = WIN EC; Qantas Credit Union/Sydney SuperDome = Qudos Bank Arena; Derwent EC = MyState Bank Arena). Where a page's link target and printed name disagreed I trusted the printed name.
- Source typos fixed from team game counts: 2010-11 NZ Breakers v Gold Coast (page says 2020), 2022-23 Perth v NZ (2022-01-10 -> 2023), 2026-27 SE Melbourne v Tasmania (2026-01-06 -> 2027). Left out: 2026-27 "Bullets Bullets" v Adelaide (club name garbled, scheduled game).
- 2020-21 Cairns home games are at "Cairns Pop-Up Arena" (16 games): no coordinates found, venue record uses Cairns city centre (approximate).

## Gaps / Unlockable
- NBL before 2006-07 is out of range. 2027 finals not played.
- sched3-espn/unresolved-venues.json (1,321 venues, all soccer plus NFL/UFL) was not handled here: none of those leagues is in this scope.
- Unlockable (no allowed source found for these leagues; counts of catalog teams still without any data, see COVERAGE.md): domestic European basketball (about 150 clubs) and handball (about 150): API-Football or Sportradar/Genius (paid), league sites with unclear reuse terms; hockey AHL/ECHL/SPHL/FPHL, KHL, Liiga, SHL, DEL, Czech, Swiss, Slovak and other European hockey (about 350 clubs): official league sites / paid feeds; NPB (12), KBO (10), CPBL (6), Frontier/Pioneer/AAPB (37): league sites, terms not cleared; MLS NEXT Pro (27) and MASL (10): league sites; Welsh, Irish, Northern Irish, Finnish, Maltese, Serbian/Croatian/Bosnian second tiers (about 100): API-Football (paid) or federation sites.

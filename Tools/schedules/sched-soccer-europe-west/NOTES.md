# sched-soccer-europe-west notes (built 2026-10-17/18)
Totals: 61 league/cup folders, 850 season files, 112,550 games; 1,856 games have no venue. Validator: 0 errors, 32 warnings (missing seasons, listed below).
Source mix (files): ESPN site API 527, ESPN + football-data.co.uk 42 (fd games added where ESPN lacked them), football-data.co.uk only 17 (fin.1 2012-26, sui.1/irl.1 2026), Wikipedia football boxes 246 (+18 merged into ESPN cups).
ESPN works (year query `dates=YYYY`, limit=1000; date RANGES return HTTP 400 now) for: eng.1/2/fa/league_cup/charity, sco.1/tennents/cis, wal.1, nir.1, irl.1, esp.1/copa/super_cup, por.1/taca, fra.1/coupe/super_cup, ger.1/dfb_pokal/super_cup, ita.1/coppa/super_cup, ned.1/cup/supercup, bel.1, aut.1, sui.1, nor.1, swe.1, den.1, mlt.1, plus relegation playoffs. ESPN has nothing for fin.1, isl.1, lux.1, bel/sui/aut/nor/swe/den/wal/nir/irl/mlt cups.
Season = start year (event season.year). Calendar-year leagues (nor, swe, fin, isl, irl) use the calendar year.
Season lengths audited per team (eng.1/esp.1/ita.1 38, ger.1/ned.1 34, fra.1 38 then 34, por.1 30/34, sco.1 38, big five all exact). Outliers are playoff/promotion games included in a league file (teams with 1-2 games), COVID-curtailed 2019 seasons (wal/nir/irl/sco/ned/fra/bel), and ESPN gaps below.
GAPS (no workable free per-game source): isl.1 and lux.1 league (all years); mlt.1 2011-15 and 2025-26; wal.1 2025-26 (ESPN stale after 2024-25 start); nir.1 2026 and the second half of 2025 (ESPN only 54 games); nor.1 2011 (ESPN 133 of 240, football-data starts 2012); fin.1 2011; sui.1 2026 has results only up to Oct 2026, no future fixtures; irl.1 2026 and fin.1 2026 are results-only (football-data has no unplayed fixtures, so those two lack "S" games).
Cups with partial rounds only: Wikipedia football boxes carry venues but pages for many cups list early rounds as tables without stadiums, so only boxed matches are included. Thin: wal.cup (finals/semis), nir.irish_cup, sui.cup (mostly finals), lux.cup, isl.cup (several years final only), bel.cup (round 6 onward), swe.cup 2024+ (qualifying/final only), den.cup 2011-14. Out of scope and not included: Scottish Challenge Cup, EFL Trophy, Welsh/Irish league cups, Belgian/Dutch second tiers. wal.cup 2014-2020 and swe.cup 2011 have no boxes/page. Coupe de France and Copa del Rey 2011-13 (and Taca de Portugal 2011-20) were merged with ESPN; duplicates removed by date+score+team/venue match, a few residual duplicates are possible.
football-data.co.uk times are UK local time, converted to UTC; where a time is missing the note says "time unknown" (12:00 local). Wikipedia times converted from the stated zone, else the country zone.
Venue inference (flagged in each file's `source`): ESPN leaves venue empty for some seasons (fra.1 2020, den.1 2019/21, por.1, bel.1 small clubs, 9 other leagues); those league games take the home club's modal venue of the same season (>=90%, >=5 games) or of the adjacent seasons (both neighbours agree). fd-only fin.1 uses the Wikipedia season "stadia" table (2017 table not parsed; Rovaniemi/TPS/Jaro some years left null). Cups are never inferred.
Team ids: ESPN ids where ESPN has the club; football-data/Wikipedia clubs matched by name to the ESPN id of the same league, otherwise `fd-<slug>` / `wp-<slug>`. Some ESPN clubs carry two ids (e.g. Mjallby, Lausanne); not merged.
Venues: venues-seen.json has 6,556 records (116 more venues, 954 games, could not be located and are omitted: DS Arena DK, Luxol Stadium MT, Energi Nord Arena DK and tiny Danish/Irish/Icelandic cup grounds; see games' venue tables). 656 match the app catalog; missing-venues.json has 2,568 venues (validator 0 errors) = venues outside the catalog with a reliable coordinate.
Coordinate sources (coordSource): catalog 656, teams-missing-venues 318 (reused from Tools/teams/soccer-europe-missing-venues.json), OSM Photon single/verified/fuzzy/team (3,412+56+144+60+4), approximate=true for 1,906 records: osm-weak 946 (OSM sports ground in the right city, name not confirmed), city-centre 905 (town centre only), team-city 49, 6 disputed. Approximate ones are NOT in missing-venues.json. Wikidata was not used (SPARQL rate limits); no second-source cross-check for osm-single.
ESPN venue id 2654 is shared by two different grounds (Meadowbank Edinburgh / Dorking); venues-seen keeps the Edinburgh record. A few ESPN venue countries are wrong (Luxol Stadium tagged JP, Guido Biondi US).
Re-run: scripts and caches live in the session scratchpad (sched/), not in the repo.

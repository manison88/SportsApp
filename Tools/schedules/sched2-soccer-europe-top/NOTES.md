# sched2-soccer-europe-top (built 2026-10-19)
Scope: European soccer tiers 1-5 of ENG, ESP, ITA, GER, FRA, SCO, NED, POR, BEL, TUR, GRE, AUT, SUI, DEN, SWE, NOR. 325 season files, 29 league folders, 114,964 games. Validator: 0 errors, 21 warnings (all "seasons missing 2011..2026" for leagues where round 1 already holds 2011+, plus the gaps below).
Audit (21 catalog leagues, 360 catalog teams): teams with a file in their own league: 282 before, 359 after (FC Vaduz has no ESPN id and no feed). Team-seasons 2006-2026 covered (same country, any tier): 5,050 before, 6,369 of 7,560 after (round 1 + sched2 + this scope; sched3-espn not counted).
Sources: ESPN site API (year queries, via Tools/schedules/fetch_espn.py) for 2006-2010 of the 21 catalog leagues, all seasons of esp.2/ita.2/ger.2/fra.2 (round 1 had none), and eng.3/4/5, sco.2/3/4, ned.2 (all 21 seasons). football-data.co.uk CSV (E0..G1, 2006-2025) as cross-check and fill. OpenLigaDB (bl3) for ger.3 2012-2026. OSM Nominatim for venue coordinates (<=1 req/s, descriptive UA).
Fixes to the raw ESPN feed (the same defects exist in sched3-espn copies of these leagues, which duplicate 165 of these files; prefer these):
- eng.1 2009: ESPN returned every match twice (ids >= 500000 carry wrong scores); removed those 357 rows.
- Postponed/other stubs (status P/O/S) that duplicate a played game of the same pair within 3-60 days removed (~700 rows, esp.2/por/tur/fra.2/ita.2 2006-07 mostly); null-venue 0-0 ghosts in esp.2 2010 removed.
- esp.2 2010 and 2011: ESPN has no Real Valladolid games; 79 games added from football-data.co.uk. ger.2 2023: ESPN stops after matchday 7; 180 games added from football-data (UK times converted to UTC). 274 games added in total, ids "fd-<date>-<home>-<away>", venue inferred, "time unknown" at 12:00Z where the CSV has no time (pre-2019 files).
- 46 ESPN games stuck as S/P/O but played were completed from football-data (not Reus Deportiu 2018-19, annulled results stay P).
- Venue inference: ESPN leaves venue empty on ~1,300 games (ita.2 2011-12, esp.2 2010-11, ned.2, swe/nor, small clubs). Filled with the home club's modal ground in the same season (>=90%, >=5 games) or nearest seasons within 3 years agreeing; flagged in each file's `source`. 743 games stay without venue.
- ger.3 (3. Liga) from OpenLigaDB: finals only (resultTypeID 2) so 2012-2026; 2008-2011 skipped (result type ambiguous), 2022 partial (211 of 380 listed by the API), 2026 results to date. Venues inferred from the club's ger.1/ger.2 ground when known, else empty. Teams matched to ESPN ids by name, others "ol-<name>" (team-names.json).
Gaps (not fixable from allowed sources): swe.1 2006-2007 and the first half of 2008 (ESPN has 131 of 240 games for 2008); nor.1 2006-2007 (football-data extras start 2012, Wikipedia only has date-less matrices); sco.3/sco.4 2025 partial (ESPN stale, 39-40 games), sco.3/4 2026 absent; ger.3 2006-2011; 2nd-tier feeds for aut/sui/den/swe/nor/tur/gre/por/bel/ita.3/esp.3/fra.3 do not exist on ESPN.
Unlockable: Turkey 1. Lig, Greece Super League 2, Portugal Liga 2, Belgium Challenger Pro, Austria 2. Liga, Swiss Challenge League, Danish 1st Division, Swedish Superettan, Norwegian OBOS-ligaen, Serie C, Primera RFEF/Segunda B, Championnat National, 3. Liga 2006-2011: only API-Football (paid) or Sofascore (not allowed); catalog teams affected: Amed, Corum, Erzurumspor, Eyupspor, Kocaelispor, Samsunspor, Kalamata, Kifisia, Volos, Ceuta, Eldense, Andorra, Burgos, Real Sociedad B, Celta Fortuna, Arezzo, Carrarese, Mantova, Rodez, Annecy and about 40 more in earlier seasons.
Uncertain: 19 games where ESPN and football-data disagree on the score were left as ESPN (e.g. ita.1 2007 Siena-Sampdoria 0-0 vs 1-2, fra.1 2006 three games, tur.1 2010 Kayserispor-Gaziantepspor, ita.2 2024 Cittadella-Pisa 0-3 vs 1-1, esp.2 2020 Alcorcon-Zaragoza 0-3 vs 0-0; several look like forfeits or ESPN placeholders). Player-level duplicates of the same club under two ESPN ids may remain (ned.1 2006-07 playoff clubs, ger.1 2008-10 relegation playoff). Lower-tier season sizes vary with playoffs included (eng.2-5 include promotion playoffs).
Venues: venues-seen.json 1,400 records (381 catalog, 872 reused coordinates from earlier scopes/Tools/venues, 84 OSM name-matched, 63 OSM stadium-in-city flagged approximate=true and left out of missing-venues.json). missing-venues.json 712 venues, validator 0 errors (one coordinate with 2 decimals: wildparkstadion, prior-scope value). 254 venues (11,247 games, mostly eng.3-5 renamed grounds and old 2006-10 stadiums) could not be located; listed in unlocated-venues.json. ESPN venue names had mojibake in 46 places, repaired.
Not used: openfootball (no venues, GitHub API blocked; nothing ESPN lacked in scope), Wikipedia, football-data.org.

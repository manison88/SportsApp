# sched-soccer-americas notes
Source: ESPN site API, one request per league-year (`scoreboard?dates=YYYY&limit=1000`; date ranges return HTTP 400 for soccer, single days and whole years work; spot-checked against single-day queries). Fetched 2026-10-17. 31 leagues, 401 season files, 86,898 games (84,646 F, 1,273 S, 539 C, 224 O, 216 P). Team ids = ESPN ids (all 2026 domestic teams match the existing teams files).
Season year: ESPN's own season label for domestic leagues (start year for split seasons: Argentina, Australia, Chile 2013-16, Uruguay, Venezuela, Bolivia, Saudi, Japan 2026-27 in 2026; Brazil 2020 includes Jan-Feb 2021). Liga MX = start-year rule (Jul-Jun: Apertura + Clausura) so 2011 file starts Jul 2011. Continental/international tournaments = edition year (date year; CWC and CCL use ESPN label). Events before 2011-season start are dropped (Jan-Jun 2011 tails: arg 191, aus 52, ccl 14, mex 167, uru 121, ven 155).
Leagues with no ESPN data for some seasons (nothing invented):
- Canada (`can.1`, CPL): ESPN has no feed at all. `usa.nwsl` 2013+; `usa.usl.1` 2017+ (2011-16 none); `ksa.1` 2022+ only, and 2022-23 has 241 of 306 games; `usa.open` 2014-2020, 2022-26 (2011-13 none, 2014 only 3 games, 2021 none; 2020 rows are cancelled).
- `concacaf.leagues.cup` 2019, 2021, 2023-26 (2020 all TBD placeholders, skipped; 2022 none). `concacaf.champions` 2011-16, 2018-26 (2016-17 edition is the 2016 file; no 2017 file). `fifa.cwc` no 2024. `fifa.world` 2014/18/22/26, `fifa.wwc` 2011/15/19/23, `concacaf.gold` odd years, `conmebol.america` 2011/15/16/19/21/24.
- `fifa.worldq.*` only qualifying years (afc/caf/concacaf/conmebol cycles); coverage by ESPN is partial (CONCACAF 2018 cycle and 2019-20 missing).
Cleaning: dropped NWSL 2014-15 duplicate Flash ghost events (25), 5 other same-pair duplicates, 17 Brazilian lower-league events inside arg.1 2012, MLS All-Star games, TBD placeholders (26). Past games ESPN left as "scheduled"/"in progress" (old NWSL) are status "O" with null scores.
Venues: 639 games have no venue in ESPN (null); 1,133 domestic-league games with missing venue got the home team's usual ground (>=90% share same season, or >=95% adjacent seasons), marked "Venue inferred (home ground)" in `note` (arg 302, bol 219, mex 205, ksa 131, par 75, nwsl 57, uru 38, ...). Shown in league-season files, not in ESPN. Neutral-site games carry "Neutral site".
venues-seen.json: 2,138 records (league x stadium; 1,366 distinct physical stadiums, renamed ids merged within 120 m, `sourceVenueIds`/`aliases` list them). 757 matched to the app catalog (`catalogVenueId`; coordinates copied from the catalog). 963 not in catalog -> missing-venues.json (validator 0 errors). Coordinates: Wikidata P625 (778, cross-checked with OSM Nominatim within 300 m for 406), OSM/Photon search (~780), curated or hand-placed (~50). `geo` field records the method.
Approximate: 81 records carry `approximate: true` (city centroid, or same ground as another id); they are NOT in missing-venues.json. 54 low-use venue ids (130 games: placeholders like "TBC", "Sin confirmar", "DOHA", tiny college fields, some AFC/CAF qualifier grounds) have no usable coordinates, so they are in the season files' venue tables but not in venues-seen.json.
Known caveats: ESPN reuses one venue id for Weidner Field (placed at the current downtown ground); ESPN gives wrong cities for a few (Barcelona SC and Emelec grounds say Quito; Barcelona's corrected to Guayaquil); Argentine 2018-19 and Bolivian 2021-24 venues are mostly inferred. Nominatim picks for small Latin American grounds were accepted only if the name or city matched; spot-check before merging into the catalog.
Scripts and caches live in the scratchpad, not the repo.

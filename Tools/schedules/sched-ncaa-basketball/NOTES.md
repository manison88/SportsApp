# sched-ncaa-basketball notes
Source: ESPN site API scoreboard, one request per day (dates=YYYYMMDD&groups=50&limit=1000; date ranges are rejected with 400), 28 Oct to 20 Apr for every season 2011-2026, men's and women's. Fetched 2026-10-17.
Files: basketball-mens-college-basketball/ and basketball-womens-college-basketball/, split by month of start UTC (<season>-<MM>.json, each ~150 KB; all well under 5 MB). season = year the season starts (2025 = 2025-26).
Counts: men's 96,224 games, women's 90,619 (186,843 total). Per season ~5,700-6,400 men's / ~5,400-6,050 women's (2020 = 2020-21, COVID: 5,282 / 4,825, with many P/C games).
Status: F 171,417; S 11,777 (all 2026-27, which has the full scheduled slate); P postponed 1,459; C cancelled 2,052 (mostly COVID 2019-20 tournament cancellations and 2020-21); O 24.
Included: regular season, conference tournaments, NCAA, NIT, CBI, CIT and other postseason (season.type 2 and 3). Excluded: ESPN season.type 1 (preseason/exhibitions).
Non-D1 opponents: groups=50 INCLUDES games that have at least one D1 team against non-D1 opponents (e.g. D2/NAIA/D3 teams). They are kept consistently, with their ESPN team id. Teams with 20+ games per season (~345 in 2011 to ~365 in 2025) are the D1 members; non-D1 teams have 1-3 games.
Dropped: 264 games whose side is the ESPN placeholder "TBD" (team ids -1/-2: future NCAA bracket slots and the cancelled 2020 tournaments) and 10 empty ESPN events (no competition/season data).
Neutral sites: note carries "(Neutral site)", plus the ESPN round headline for tournaments (e.g. "Men's Basketball Championship - West Region - 1st Round"); venues are ESPN's per-game venues (NCAA host arenas correct in spot checks). home = ESPN designated home.
VENUE GAPS: ESPN has no venue for ~19% of 2011-12 (men's 2011-2012, women's 2011-2012) and a few later games. For non-neutral games with no venue I used the HOME TEAM'S HOME ARENA (ESPN team.venue); those games have "venue = home arena (inferred)" in the note (about 4,200 games). Neutral games without a venue have venueId null (45 games: 71+42 final with no venue incl. those).
Season length check: max games for a team per season is 33-42 (e.g. UK 40, Syracuse 42, Villanova 41, UConn W 40); no D1 outliers. 2019 (2019-20) and 2020 (2020-21) are shortened by COVID.
Team ids are ESPN ids; no college basketball teams exist in the app's teams files yet, so ids line up only with ESPN (externalIds.espn + espnLeague basketball/mens-college-basketball or womens-...).
Venue ids are shared between the men's and women's leagues; venues-seen.json has one record per (league, ESPN venue id): 1,496 records (887 distinct ESPN venue ids). 381 records match the app catalog (name+city, or <=400 m); 679 distinct venues are in missing-venues.json.
Coordinates (coordQuality in venues-seen): catalog 381, osm 653 (OpenStreetMap Nominatim hit on the arena name inside the right state), wikidata 33 (P625, within 25 km of the city), campus 286 (APPROXIMATE: university campus centroid of the home school, probably within ~500 m of the arena), city 143 (APPROXIMATE: city centre, kilometres off possible). Nominatim hits were checked only for state, not individually: a few same-name mismatches are possible, and ~29 coordinate pairs are shared by two ESPN venue names (mostly one building under two names). Place Bell coordinates are from memory.
Missing-venues: sports basketball, homeTeams [] (not available), capacity/opened null, timeZone from state with longitude splits (approximate for border towns). Non-US venues (Bahamas, Puerto Rico, Mexico, Canada, Europe, Asia) have real country codes; ESPN gives no country so these were mapped from the state field.
Venue names: ESPN's current name is used for all seasons (naming-rights changes are not tracked per game).

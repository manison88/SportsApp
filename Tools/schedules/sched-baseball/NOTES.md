Counts: 41,152 / 31,566 / 31,012 / 30,958 / 41,114.
- MiLB 2020 cancelled: no files. Short-season A (sportId 15) and independent/winter leagues not included.
Handling: a game postponed and replayed appears once (latest date, same gamePk). Final statuses F; unplayed in the future S (4 MLB ALCS games); postponed and never replayed P; cancelled C (MLB 11, MiLB mostly cancelled playoff games). "Completed Early"/suspended games are F with score.
Placeholders dropped: games with a TBD/placeholder team (2026 World Series "TB/CLE" TBD games not in file; MiLB cancelled playoff games with the same TBD team on both sides).
Notes field: playoff round (MLB: Wild Card, Division Series, League Championship Series, World Series; MiLB: seriesDescription). "Neutral site" is added when a team's home venue accounts for <8% of its home games that season (e.g. London/Seoul/Tokyo, 2020-21 Blue Jays in Buffalo/Dunedin, bubble postseason), plus the statsapi description for MLB.
Spot checks: MLB every team plays 160-163 regular-season games in 2011-2019, 2021-2025 (2026 156-162 incl. unplayed, no outliers); 2020 56-64. MiLB per-team counts match the usual 140/138/132 (Triple-A 144; Mexican League ~120). No outliers needing action.
Venues: venues-seen.json = 399 rows (313 distinct statsapi venues: 50 MLB, 73/46/73/67 MiLB 11/12/13/14, 90 Rookie); 45 rows matched to the app catalog (distance <= 400 m). missing-venues.json = 277 venues (validator 0 errors); timeZone/capacity from statsapi, opened = null, id slugs derived from name + city.
Coordinates: 266 rows straight from statsapi defaultCoordinates (cross-checked against city geocode, no outliers). 117 rows had none in statsapi and are coordSource "nominatim-approx" (OSM Nominatim by venue name/address, accepted only if within 40 km of the city, typically ~100 m but not individually verified). 16 are "manual" (OSM lookups of the actual stadium, or Bristol Motor Speedway corrected from a wrong statsapi point). Treat nominatim-approx rows as approximate; Mexican/Dominican/spring-complex rows are the least certain; Giants Baseball Complex was mapped to Scottsdale Stadium, Parque Domingo Santana (Leon) to the city centre.
Gaps: 33 venues with no findable coordinates are left out of venues and their games have venueId null: statsapi placeholder "TBD" (id 401) and "Generic VST" (4270) = all DSL games (about 21,400 Rookie games with no venue), the Dominican "<Team> DR Complex" academies, Ballpark of the Palm Beaches (6022/6084), Jack Russell Memorial Stadium (2525), Fort Bragg Field (5010; 1 MLB game), Felipe Alou academy. Turner Field is kept as its own missing venue (catalog only has the rebuilt Center Parc Stadium).

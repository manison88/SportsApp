# sched-tournaments-soccer (fetched 2026-10-18)
Sources: ESPN site API scoreboard (dates=YYYY and YYYYMM per league key, 2010-2027) for 61 soccer keys (one empty); every season then compared match-by-match with Wikipedia match boxes (season / qualification pages) and patched; venues from sibling soccer agents' venues-seen (same ESPN venue ids), the catalog, OSM (Photon/Nominatim) and Wikipedia coordinates. Totals: 60 league folders, 365 season files, 37,001 games, 2,406 venues (429 in catalog, 1,977 in missing-venues.json). Own slug: `soccer/uefa.weuroq` (UEFA Women's Euro qualifying, Wikipedia only). `source` is `espn`, `espn+wikipedia` or `wikipedia` per file.
Season rules: start year for club cups; edition year for finals tournaments (Euro/Olympics 2020 and Copa America 2021 are filed 2020/2020/2021, AFCON 2021 = Jan 2022, CWC 2022/2023 = Feb/Dec 2023, no CWC 2024 exists); qualifiers by cycle start (WCQ 2011/2015/2019/2023, women's WCQ 2013/2017/2021/2025, Euro qualifying 2010/2014/2018/2022: the 2010 file is the only pre-2011 season, kept because Euro 2012 qualifying ran Sep 2010-Nov 2011). CAF CL/Confed season 2018 holds two editions (calendar-2018 and 2018-19).
Completeness (all match the official totals): WC 64/64/64/104 (2014/18/22/26, 2026 all F); WWC 32/52/52/64 (2027 not scheduled: ESPN has no fixtures and the draw is not made, nothing added); Euro 31/51/51/51; Women's Euro 25/31/31/31 (2013 added from Wikipedia); Copa America 26/26/32/26/28/32; Gold Cup 25/25/26/25/31x4; AFCON 32x4 then 52x4; Asian Cup 32/32/51/51 (+51 scheduled 2027); Confed Cup 16/16; Olympics men 32 and women 26 each; CWC 8 per edition (7 in 2022/2023) and 64 in 2025 (63 + the LAFC-Club America play-in, noted as "Qualifying Play-In"); UEFA Super Cup 1 per season; Nations League 142/166/172/188/156 (2026-27 in progress).
Club cups: for UCL, UEL and UECL every season's total (qualifying + play-off + main) equals the Wikipedia match-box count; every team plays 6 group games or 8/6 league-phase games (zero outliers, 2026-27 league phase scheduled). ESPN gaps fixed from Wikipedia: UEL 2013-14 (39 matches missing), AFC Champions League 2011-2015 (2015 had 23 of 143), all CAF CL/Confed preliminary and first rounds (1,748 matches), UWCL 2011-2018 entirely plus qualifying 2019+ (1,392), Women's WCQ UEFA 2013/2017 cycles, UEFA Women's Nations League 2023-24, CNL 2018 qualifying, AFCON qualifying 2011/2014, WCQ intercontinental play-offs 2013/2017/2022, and ~25 single fixtures (ECU-BOL 2012, CAN-SKN 2011, RSA-SEN 2016, OFC Nations Cup rounds...). 1,677 existing rows were corrected (stale or wrong scores/dates, mostly UEFA qualifying 2011-13 and AFC) and 14 wrong duplicate rows dropped. 4,973 games are Wikipedia-sourced (event ids start with "w"); their kick-off times are Wikipedia local times converted with the host country's zone (approximate by up to 1 h; many ESPN qualifier rows carry a 17:00Z placeholder time).
Teams: ESPN ids; 653 clubs absent from ESPN (CAF/UWCL/AFC early rounds) use ids `x:<slug>` with a 3-letter abbreviation. Women's national teams have their own ESPN ids.
Notes: round names normalised; extra time "aet", shoot-outs "pens h-a" (home-away order), "Neutral site" where ESPN flags it. Past fixtures never resulted are status O ("result not recorded", 48); Leagues Cup 2020 is C. 241 games have no venue (source gives none, or the venue could not be located).
Gaps: no fixtures for CONCACAF Gold Cup qualifying before 2020, Asian Cup 2011 and AFCON 2012 qualifying (cycles started in 2010), women's WCQ outside UEFA, Libertadores/CCL/Sudamericana qualifying pages not cross-checked beyond ESPN totals; CAF/UWCL early-round club names were matched to ESPN by name similarity (some could still be split across two ids).
Venue coordinates: 1,708 from sibling agents/catalog/local research files (Wikidata or catalog based), 106 where OSM and Nominatim/Wikipedia agree within 400 m, 194 single-source OSM/Wikipedia/Nominatim with matching name and place, 399 flagged `approximate: true` (city- or weak-name-level, mostly small CAF/AFC/women's-cup grounds; a benchmark against the sibling coordinates puts single-source OSM within 500 m for ~85% of venues, so treat approximate ones as city-level). Not independently confirmed with Wikidata P625 (Wikimedia API rate limit); 5 venues (11 games) could not be located and carry venueId null. ESPN venue country/city are unreliable for small grounds; countryCode was re-derived from coordinates where the neighbouring trusted venues disagreed.
Validator: 0 errors (53 warnings = leagues with fewer than 16 seasons, expected). Tools/venues validator on missing-venues.json: 0 errors, 17 warnings (coordinates with few decimals for manually placed grounds).

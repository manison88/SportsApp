# us-pro-history notes (scope: MLB, NFL, NBA, NHL, WNBA, MLS, NWSL, CFL, 2011-2026)
Validator: `validate.py us-pro-history` -> 32 teams, 9 leagues, 75 missing venues, 0 errors; venues/validate.py on the venues file: 0 errors.
Files: us-pro-history-{teams,leagues,missing-venues,fixes}.json. No existing file was edited.
## Verification of current teams (job 1)
Method: every current team's 2025/2026 home games from the ESPN schedule API (home venue by game count), coordinates vs Wikidata (150 m rule) for ~150 venues.
Result: no coordinate over 150 m wrong (Wikidata is the coarser side for SoFi 348 m, BMO Stadium 155 m, Nu Stadium, Centennial Stadium: catalog values are right). Rosters complete: NFL 32, NHL 32, MLB 30, NBA 30, WNBA 15, MLS 30, NWSL 16, CFL 9; no 2026-27 expansion/relocation is missing.
us-pro-history-fixes.json (48 entries, all sure): Rate Field and NRG Stadium names; mlb-cle Indians -> Guardians (3 files); bc-place wrongly carries the Canucks/hockey/Rogers Arena aliases; wnba-dal home is College Park Center (21 of 25 home games in 2026), not American Airlines Center; additive leagues/alias patches (NFL/MLB/NBA/NHL neutral sites, Turner Field = center-parc-stadium, Tulsa Shock at bok-center, Dream at mccamish, Storm at hec-edmundson, FC Cincinnati at nippert).
Not changed on purpose: Tampa Bay Rays used Steinbrenner Field in 2025 only (back at Tropicana in 2026); Atlanta Dream split 2026 between Gateway Center and State Farm Arena; Chicago Fire 2025 had one game at SeatGeek Stadium; mls-nyc/mls-rbny/etc. fine. ESPN colors differ for ~30 current teams (not touched; colors were out of scope).
## History (job 2)
32 team records `hist-<league>-<city>-<name>` (NFL 5, NHL 4, MLB 4, NBA 4, WNBA 3, MLS 3, NWSL 8, CFL 1), `tier` 1, `active {from,to}` = calendar years played under that identity within 2011-2026, ESPN id of the era (names/colors from ESPN season API). The franchises that moved or renamed keep the SAME ESPN id as the current team (Raiders 13, Chargers 24, Rams 14, Commanders 28, Jets/Thrashers 28, Coyotes 24, A's 11, Marlins 28, Guardians 5, Pelicans 3, Hornets 30, Nets 17, Wings 3, Aces 17, CF Montreal 9720, Courage 15366 ...): the seed merge key (espnLeague, espn) WILL clash with the current teams. Keep history teams out of that index or key them with active.
leagueKey is `history/<league>` (nfl, nhl, mlb, nba, wnba, mls, nwsl, cfl) to avoid (leagueKey, abbreviation) clashes with current teams (WSH, OAK, CHA...) and to keep current Passport checklists clean; plus an umbrella record `history/us-major-historic-venues` (teamIds empty, teamCount 0, passport = all 75 new + ~27 existing historic/neutral venues).
Not recorded as separate teams (same identity, only a cosmetic name change): Houston Dynamo, Chicago Fire(FC), Utah Royals (2024 relaunch uses ESPN 19141 like the 2018-20 club, so the old club is recorded), Reign FC, Seattle Reign. Mullett/Gila River Coyotes are one franchise (two records: Phoenix 2011-14, Arizona 2014-24). Los Angeles Rams (2016-19 Coliseum) and Chargers (2017-19 StubHub) are the current franchises.
## Venues (75 in us-pro-history-missing-venues.json)
Representation of closed venues (the SPEC has none): extra fields `closed` = last season (start year) the venue hosted one of the 8 leagues, `demolished` = year or null (still standing), `note`; the validator ignores them. Years marked "unverified" in `note` come from memory, not a source.
Existing catalog ids reused (not duplicated): Turner Field = center-parc-stadium, Georgia/Philips Arena = state-farm-arena, Staples/Oracle/Kia renames, Mandalay Bay = michelob-ultra-arena, KeyArena = climate-pledge-arena, TCF Bank = huntington-bank-stadium, Mapfre/Lower.com ids, Wembley, Tottenham, Allianz, Frankfurt, Azteca, Corinthians, Bernabeu, Maracana, Stade de France, Berlin Olympiastadion, Tokyo Dome, London Stadium, Alfredo Harp Helu, Gocheok, O2 Prague, Avicii, Nokia, Uber Arena, Accor Arena, Olympic Stadium Montreal, Bobby Dodd, Camping World (patches in fixes).
Same-site rebuilds that the seed same_place dedupe will merge into a current venue: metrodome/us-bank (0 m), ivor-wynne-stadium/hamilton-stadium (31 m), drv-pnk-stadium is separate from Nu Stadium; ralph-wilson-stadium vs highmark-stadium are 435 m apart (kept separate, but a 400 m rule almost merges them).

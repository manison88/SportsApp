# us-college-football notes
Scope: FBS (138) and FCS (127) programs, 2026 membership as ESPN lists it, in 25 conference leagues (11 FBS + 14 FCS incl. both Independents). Team ids `cfb-<ESPN abbreviation>` (e.g. cfb-mich; Miami OH = cfb-moh, Texas A&M = cfb-tamu, William & Mary = cfb-wm). tier 1 = FBS, tier 2 = FCS.
Validator: 265 teams, 25 leagues, 120 missing venues, 0 errors (venues validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings).
Leagues: `leagueKey` football/college-football/<slug>, `espnPath` null (ESPN has no per-conference feed); every team keeps `externalIds.espnLeague` = football/college-football. One passport per conference ("SEC stadiums") with one entry per distinct home venue. Season label "Aug-Jan".
Membership source: ESPN core API `seasons/2026/types/2/groups/{80,81}` children + each group's `teams` (the site-API standings omit Sun Belt and SWAC). Pac-12 = 8 (Boise St, Colorado St, Fresno St, Oregon St, San Diego St, Texas St, Utah St, Washington St). Sacramento State is in the MAC, North Dakota State in the Mountain West, Delaware/JSU/Kennesaw/Missouri State in C-USA, per ESPN.
Teams: locationName = ESPN `location` (school), teamName = ESPN nickname, colors from ESPN `color`/`alternateColor`.
Colors NOT from ESPN (ESPN had no secondary, or black/none, filled from Wikipedia school colors, approximate): Utah Tech, Towson, Maine, Campbell, Hampton, Monmouth, Stony Brook, Chicago State, Merrimack, Stonehill, LIU, Mercyhurst, Lindenwood, Valparaiso, St. Thomas, UTRGV, Houston Christian, McNeese, East Texas A&M, Austin Peay, North Alabama, Tarleton State, Incarnate Word, San Diego. Least certain: Merrimack, LIU, Mercyhurst, Lindenwood, St. Thomas, UTRGV. Several ESPN primaries are black (Army, Cincinnati, UCF, Vanderbilt, App State, ...): kept as ESPN gives them; `accent` is the readable secondary.
Founded = founding year of the school: Wikidata inception (name+state match, 232 of 265) or, for 33, my own knowledge (not verified on the web, listed in build as manual): Cal Poly, Georgetown, Howard, Southern Illinois, UMass, North Carolina, Bowling Green, Furman, Washington, Stonehill, Lafayette, Illinois, UAlbany, Air Force, App State, Austin Peay, Central Connecticut, Davidson, Liberty, MTSU, Navy, Nicholls, Presbyterian, Sam Houston, SE Louisiana, SFA, Wagner, Wofford, Merrimack, Florida, Kentucky, Washington State, Louisiana. `externalIds.wikidata` is the SCHOOL's Q id (not the team's) and is null for 33 teams where the match was manual or missing.
Venues: ESPN's venue per team (core API) was stale for ~20 programs and was replaced by the current stadium: UAB Protective Stadium, Baylor McLane, Houston TDECU, Rutgers SHI, UNLV Allegiant, SDSU Snapdragon, Colorado State Canvas, Northwestern Ryan Field (new, in Tools/venues/us-college-football.json), Hawai'i Ching Complex (Aloha Stadium closed), Cal Poly Alex G. Spanos Stadium (ESPN had a wrong Maryland entry), Illinois Gies Memorial (ESPN had Foster Stadium, VA), South Dakota State Dana J. Dykhouse, UAlbany Casey Stadium, Bethune-Cookman Daytona Stadium, Prairie View Panther Stadium (OSM had a wrong Odessa hit), UAPB Golden Lion, Alcorn Casem-Spinks, Morehead Jayne Stadium (Phil Simms Stadium), Monmouth Kessler Stadium, McNeese Cowboy Stadium.
Reused: 149 teams use venues already in the catalog / Tools/venues (matched by name + state; football-capable venues only, arenas excluded because several catalog arenas carry stray aliases like "Alumni Stadium"). 5 reused venues exist only outside the catalog (finley-stadium, seatgeek-stadium, torero-stadium, ryan-field, memorial-stadium-indiana) and are copied unchanged into the missing-venues file so the validator passes; the build dedupes them by id.
New venues: 115 with coordinates from OpenStreetMap Nominatim (stadium polygon, 1 req/s) or Wikipedia (Fitton Field, Vackar, Saxon, Bailey, Beirne, Daytona Stadium, Wagner, Bethpage FCU, Casem-Spinks, Husky Stadium HCU). 85 were cross-checked against Wikipedia page coordinates (nearly all within 120 m; Casey Stadium 257 m, Five Star Stadium 276 m, Braly 202 m: OSM point is the stadium, Wikipedia point is the complex). capacity/opened come from Wikipedia infoboxes where the page matched (85 / 54 filled), else null.
Approximate / uncertain venues: Tarleton "Tarleton Memorial Stadium" (OSM Memorial Stadium, Stephenville), Rice Stadium (ESPN "First Community Stadium"), Tennessee State (ESPN: Nissan Stadium), Texas Tech Galaxy Stadium (ESPN name for Jones AT&T Stadium), Daytona Stadium (Bethune-Cookman) taken from Wikipedia only. City fields: Villanova, Homewood (Samford), Hillsboro (Portland State), Brookville (LIU), Albany differ from ESPN's city on purpose.
Skipped: West Florida (UAC 2026 newcomer): Pen Air Field has no OSM or Wikipedia coordinates, so the team was left out rather than guessed.
Not done (stretch): Division II (ESPN group 57, ~170 programs), Division III (group 58, ~240) and NAIA. ESPN lists them but ~250 of those have no team colors, and venue data (stale names, wrong states, isActive=false for ~100 teams) would need per-school hand verification; excluded to keep the quality bar. Group ids and a membership dump are reproducible from the ESPN core API above.
Shared by name only: chicago-state plays at seatgeek-stadium (Bridgeview, a soccer venue record); its football tenant is not added to that record.

# Printed keepsakes (parked Oct 19, 2026)

Physical things made from the fan's record. Three rounds of mockups, all rendered from the sample library (Sam, 60 games, 2014–2026) with the app's own stamps, team colors and fonts, plus the owner's USA v BiH photos. Nothing here is in the app. The SwiftUI that rendered them lived in throwaway worktrees and was not kept; the renders are in `png/`.

## Round 3, book formats — the direction to pick up (`png/book-formats/`)

The book as an object the fan works on over the years, not pages to read. Each is built on the full league (every current MLB park), with the fan's grounds filled in and the rest waiting.

| # | Format | One line | Price idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scratch-off passport | Every park under silver foil; yours arrive scratched in the team's color, the rest you scratch with the coin in the sleeve the night you go. | $59 |

Owner's steer: the scratch-off and sticker album are the two that sell to someone *before* they have a history. Sticker album has the cleanest recurring revenue.

## Round 1, the passport book and tickets (`png/keepsakes/`)

- Passport book: navy cloth hardcover, passport size, gold foil; spread with the stamps as if hand-pressed, "18 games · 14–4" under each.
- Hard ticket: front (score, World Cup, Levi's Stadium, tear-off "WITNESSED" stub), back (photos + QR to the game in the app).
- Season ticket set: a fanned stack of heavy ticket cards for one ground's season, in a keepsake box.
- Yearly sports passport: a shelf of yearly editions, one cloth color a year; inside spreads pairing each stamp with that day's photos (2019 Wrigley; 2026 USA v BiH at Levi's).
- Full-page stamp: the ground from above fills the whole left page in the team's color, with coordinates and a north arrow; the day's photos opposite. Owner asked for this.

## Round 2, prints (`png/prints/`) — owner rejected the lot ("all trash")

Kept for the record only: scoreboard print (line score), turning point (win probability), one dot per game, collector's sheet, visa page (MRZ encodes the score), route log, then & now, boarding pass per trip, who came along, big night (foil), almanac (weather ring), seat plate, a child's first games.

## Vendors and blockers

- Books and prints: Prodigi (API, hardcover photo books, prints near the customer; smallest hardcover is A5, not true passport size). Lulu Direct as a backup for books.
- 32 pt ticket card: 4OVER4 has the stock and perforation; single-unit orders not confirmed, so batch weekly.
- **Aerial imagery is Apple Maps and cannot be printed and sold** under Apple's terms. Any aerial stamp, the collector's sheet, the route log and the fold-out map need a print-licensed source (Mapbox, Esri, Nearmap) before they ship.
- Sample caveats: "The drums came with us." and the kid's birth year (2010) are stand-ins; the sample's MLB list included one-off venues (London, Tokyo, Bristol) until filtered to US/CA parks.

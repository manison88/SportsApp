Okafor goes 6–3, conquers the cold
Nine games, three new venues and one kick through the snow that nobody in Section 112 will forget.
FROZEN IN TIME · Lambeau Field, Jan. 5, minutes after a 51-yard kick beat Green Bay 24–22 as time expired. Photo: S. Okafor, one of 212.
GREEN BAY — It started at 19 degrees and only got better. Sam Okafor, accompanied most often by daughter Maya, watched Chicago win six of nine, added three venues to a growing passport and logged 3,180 miles in pursuit. Maya’s verdict: “Same seats next year.”

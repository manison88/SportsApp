# Share cards round 2: every sport (mockups only, do not build)

Read `BRIEF.md` in this folder first (the task format, the canvas, how to render, the rules). This round **replaces** your earlier set for the experience you are given. The client's feedback: **the designs looked nice but were baseball-only, and a share card has to work for every sport**, because a fan's library mixes baseball, football, basketball, hockey, soccer and racing.

## What "works for every sport" means (hard rules)
- **No baseball-only visuals or language.** No diamond, no manual scoreboard, no innings, no "MLB parks", no "of 30". Say "venues", "stadiums", "arenas" or "grounds". Counts are the fan's own count (21 venues), not a fraction of a league.
- **The structure must be sport-neutral**: a big number or photo, a place, a date, a result. Seat/field drawings must be generic (a seating bowl or arena ring, a pitch, a track line) or adapt per sport, never a baseball field.
- **Show proof.** Each of your 6 cards uses a **different primary sport**, so the set itself proves the design works across sports: v1 soccer, v2 football, v3 basketball, v4 hockey, v5 racing (or soccer again if the experience has no racing meaning), v6 a mixed-sport card (a fan with several sports). Keep the same 3 directions x 2 treatments structure from BRIEF.md (v1/v2 direction A, v3/v4 B, v5/v6 C), but make each direction a real template that obviously drops into any sport: if direction A is a photo hero, v1 and v2 are the same template in two sports and two treatments. Describe in your report how each direction adapts to the other sports.
- Real data only, from the sample fan Sam (Chicago): 60 games, 21 venues. Sports: baseball 31 (Wrigley 18), football 10 (Soldier Field 9, Lambeau 1), soccer 7 (Soldier Field 4, Signal Iduna Park, Camp Nou, Bernabeu; Barcelona 5, Eibar 0 on Feb 22 2020), hockey 6 and basketball 5 (all at United Center), racing 1 (COTA, Oct 24 2021). Examples to use: Bears 27, Vikings 24 at Soldier Field Nov 2 2025 (NFL); Bulls 112, Heat 109 at United Center Feb 15 2026 (NBA); Blackhawks 4, Blues 1 at United Center Mar 2 2025 (NHL); Dortmund 4, Schalke 4 at Signal Iduna Park Nov 25 2017 (soccer); US Grand Prix at the Circuit of the Americas Oct 24 2021 (racing). Full data: `../../Witness/Resources/Sample/library.json`.
- City names only, no logos, no 2-3 letter team codes as monograms (the earlier cards used them; avoid). Photos: use `../photos/` (see `INDEX.md`): wrigley-*.jpg are baseball, so prefer soldier-*.jpg, lambeau-*.jpg, united-center-court.jpg, camp-nou-aerial.jpg, fenway-*.jpg only where the sport does not matter (crowd/exterior). Crop so no sport-specific field markings give away baseball.

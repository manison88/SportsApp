Idea: the game as a match report. Compact hero (best photo, "You were there", 104 / 38 / 3 story), a sticky mini-timeline (game clock with photo-density ticks, goal dots, playhead), then a vertical spine feed: Before, Kickoff, each key play as a card, Halftime, Full time, After. Quiet stretches collapse to one line ("8 quiet minutes").
Gestures: scrolling moves the playhead along the mini-timeline and updates its readout (minute, wall time, "42 s after the goal", score). Dragging the mini-timeline scrubs the feed. Tapping a thumbnail opens the photo full-screen with the same readout; Share on a photo exports it with the score at that instant. Cards with no photos stay compact.
No win-probability sports (soccer, F1): the spine and mini-timeline use only the game's own clock and key plays. Baseball/football/basketball/hockey add the WP layer as an optional fill under the mini-timeline plus a per-inning/period sparkline and a "+56 pts" swing callout. (Library data bottoms at 33% before Bryant's homer in the 4th, not 28% in the 6th; mockup follows the data.)
Degrades: with 0-2 photos and no plays the feed keeps three beats (before, the match as one dashed card with the final score and "Add a goal / Add a note", after); the timeline shows only kickoff/final and the photo ticks. With 0 photos the hero shows the venue image and "Add photos from this night".
SwiftUI: NavigationStack + ScrollView(LazyVStack) with a pinned section header (safeAreaInset or .scrollTargetLayout + onScrollGeometryChange) holding a Canvas mini-timeline; each moment is a Grid row: minute Text, spine via overlay/Path (dashed for unknown stretches), card VStack, photo strip as ScrollView(.horizontal) of LazyHStack with .scrollTargetBehavior(.viewAligned); WP sparkline with Swift Charts (AreaMark + RuleMark at 50%); photo viewer via matchedTransitionSource + fullScreenCover.

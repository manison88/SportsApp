# Replay
**Idea:** the game as a story player. Full-bleed photo, one thin segment per MOMENT (Arrival, Kickoff, 29' goal, 34' own goal, 42' goal, Halftime, 80' goal, Final whistle, After; baseball: Arrival, innings 1-9, After). A broadcast score bug at the top updates per moment; a big lower third says the minute and the play ("42' Al Haydos"); an amber "You were here · 12:47 PM" chip pins the photo's real time. Pre-kickoff uses a negative clock (-38').
**Gestures:** tap right/left = next/previous moment (photos inside a moment auto-advance); hold = pause; swipe down = close; the SCRUB pill (or dragging the dock) turns the segments into a draggable clock timeline with one tick per photo, goal dots on the phase bar, and an amber playhead. Scrub snaps to key plays with a haptic. Last frame is a recap card (photo count, during-play count, within a minute of a key play, first/last photo time) with Share.
**No win probability (soccer, F1):** the axis is only the game clock/phases plus key-play dots; WP is an optional layer. Baseball (v2) replaces the dock with the WP curve along the bottom: played part solid, future dotted, biggest swing drawn white with a "+34" callout, photo ticks and innings underneath.
**0-2 photos / no plays (v3):** segments shrink to Arrival / Match / After; the clock still runs, the bug shows the final only, the timeline shows the two photos as thumbnails and a dashed unknown-match span, with an "Add a note" invitation. With 0 photos the first frame is the generative scoreboard canvas and the story is just the recap card.
**SwiftUI sketch:** ZStack { PhotoLayer (AsyncImage + gradient overlays); VStack { SegmentBar (HStack of Capsule + GeometryReader progress), ScoreBug (HStack), Spacer, LowerThird (VStack), ScrubDock (Material, Canvas for ticks + DragGesture) } }; gestures via .gesture(LongPressGesture) for hold + SpatialTapGesture for left/right halves; WP curve is a Chart/Path in the dock; state in an @Observable ReplayModel(moments, photos, playhead: Date).

# Heartbeat
**Idea:** the fan's whole day is one chart on the game's own clock: a stepped score-difference line (soccer) or win-probability curve (baseball), an amber photo-density histogram beneath it (the "heartbeat"), goal/key-play markers, halftime as a hatched quiet gap, and generous tinted bands for arrival and leaving. Photo, score at that instant and the nearest play sit right below.
**Gestures:** drag the chart to move the playhead (snaps to photo bins, haptic tick on goals); the photo carousel swipes between photos and drags the playhead with it; tap a key play to jump to its nearest photo; share button shares the current photo with its score and minute; the star marks the best photo.
**No win probability (soccer, F1, anything):** the line is score difference from play events; with no score events it is just the histogram on the clock. Phase labels come from the sport (halves, innings, periods, quarters, race laps).
**Degrades:** 3+ photos get the full chart; 1-2 photos drop the histogram for large photo pins on a clean axis; no play data shows a quiet "no play-by-play" band and an "Add what you remember" card; 0 photos shows the axis with a "Add photos from this day" prompt.
**SwiftUI:** ZStack/VStack; chart = Swift Charts (LineMark with .interpolationMethod(.stepEnd), BarMark for density bins, RuleMark for the playhead, PointMark for plays) with chartOverlay + DragGesture; carousel = TabView(.page) or ScrollView with scrollTargetBehavior(.viewAligned); stats = HStack of three Text; key plays = LazyVStack of rows with ScrollViewReader to sync selection.

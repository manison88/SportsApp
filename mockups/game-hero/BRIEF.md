# Game page cover: icons for "You were there" and the stadium, on the cover image

Read `../BRIEF.md` first (bar, kit, fonts, rules: no real team logos, 393x852 @3x, nothing clips, check every PNG with the Read tool, 2-3 passes). Same design language (dark ink, amber `--signal`, champagne `--trophy` for title games, condensed type).

## The game page today
Opening a game from the Timeline shows a tall cover (about 60% of the screen): the game's best photo with a scrim, nav bar overlay (back arrow left, share/menu right), then bottom-left: an event badge for big games (World Series...), the headline in big condensed caps over two lines ("CHICAGO 3, / CLEVELAND 2."), a line "Wrigley Field, Sunday, October 30, 2016" and a kickoff line with weather ("7:20 PM" with a cloud icon). Everything else is BELOW the cover and needs scrolling: the score table, "I was there for" chips, photo strip, people, and the rows "You were there" (waveform icon) and the stadium row (building icon, venue name) which open the You-were-there match report and the venue page.

## What to design
Put two icon buttons for those two destinations on the RIGHT side of the info area at the bottom of the cover image, so people can reach them without scrolling: "You were there" (symbol waveform.path.ecg) and "Stadium" (symbol building.columns). Each 48-52pt touch target, glass or ink circle/rounded square with a tiny caption under the icon (YOU WERE THERE / STADIUM, 10-11pt tracked caps), aligned to the bottom-right of the info block, never overlapping the text on the left. Also show the third optional one when present (Trip: airplane).

Also fix the headline: team names and scores must ALWAYS be one line per team over the cover (never wrapping mid-name), scaling the type down dynamically for long names ("Bosnia and Herzegovina 3" must stay on one line next to Qatar 1). Format per line: team name left, score right-aligned in tabular numerals in a fixed score column, winner first, rooted team marked quietly. Show how it behaves with a short pair (Chicago 3 / Cleveland 2) and a long pair (Bosnia and Herzegovina 3 / Qatar 1).

Give THREE different variations of the bottom-right icon treatment (v1, v2, v3), for example: (1) two stacked glass circle buttons with captions, (2) a vertical pill with both icons divided by a hairline, (3) icon + label chips in a row along the info line's right edge. Pick the best layout for each; all must work on small phones (width 393) with the long-name soccer game.

Deliver `features/<dir>/` per variation named `v1-stacked-circles`, `v2-vertical-pill`, `v3-icon-chips`, each with `feature.json` ({"title","summary"}) and TWO screens: `v1-main.html` (Bosnia and Herzegovina 3, Qatar 1, Lumen Field, FIFA World Cup group stage, with the cover photo, the rest of the page peeking below the cover) and `v2-big-game.html` (2016 World Series Game 5 at Wrigley Field, Chicago 3 Cleveland 2, champagne treatment, World Series badge, trip chip optional). Kit paths from `features/<dir>/`: `../../../kit/kit.css`, `../../../kit/kit.js`, photos `../../../photos/<name>.jpg` (see `../photos/INDEX.md`; stand-in crops, avoid visible ad logos).

## Description (4000)
Witness turns your camera roll into your fan life. It finds the games you've been to from your photos, matches them to the real schedule, and builds a timeline of every night in the stands.

FIND YOUR GAMES AUTOMATICALLY
Witness looks at where and when your photos were taken. Photos at a stadium on a game day become that game: the teams, the final score, the weather at first pitch or kickoff, and the crowd. It all happens on your iPhone. Your photos are never uploaded.

RELIVE THE NIGHT
Every game gets its own page: your photos, the score, the box score and the big moments. See your photos placed on the game's own clock, so you know you snapped that picture minutes after the go-ahead goal.

COLLECT EVERY STADIUM
Your Passport gives you a stamp for every ground, park and arena you've visited, each one with the stadium from above in your team's colors. Chase all 30 ballparks, tick off the grounds on your bucket list, and plan the next one.

COUNT THE MILES
The Map draws a line from home to every venue you've been to. See how far you've traveled for the games, how many countries, and which stadiums you keep coming back to.

YOUR WHOLE FAN RECORD
Wins, losses and draws when you're in the building. Your lucky charm record. The coldest night, the biggest crowd, title games you saw in person, and the friends you went with.

PLAN WHAT'S NEXT
Pick a team, see its upcoming schedule and plan a game. Get a countdown, the forecast and a game-day reminder, and Witness adds your photos automatically after the final whistle.

SHARE THE NIGHT
Turn any game, your Passport or your Map into a story card made from your own photos and stats.

EVERY SPORT
Baseball, soccer, American football, basketball, hockey, handball and motorsport, from the big leagues to national teams, cup finals and race weekends. Add any game by hand, including your own teams and local clubs.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your photos stay on your phone. There is no account to create. Back up and export your games whenever you like.

Witness is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any league, team or venue. Team and venue names are used only to identify the games you attended.

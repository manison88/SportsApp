# Tournaments in the catalog

State of Oct 20, 2026. Written by the tournament-catalog pass (branch `claude/witness-mockups-phase1`); the data is generated, see "Regenerating".

## How the catalog works (what this pass relied on)

- The app lists seven sports (`Sport` in `Witness/Model/Models.swift`): baseball, football (American and Canadian), basketball, hockey, soccer, motorsport, handball. Rugby, cricket, tennis and volleyball are not app sports (see the last section).
- A team is one record in `Witness/Resources/Catalog/teams.json` (53 hand-kept), `teams-more.json` (generated by `Tools/teams/build_catalog.py` from the research files `Tools/teams/*-teams.json`) or `national-teams.json` (soccer men's sides, `Tools/teams/add_national.py`, ESPN ids). The picker (`TeamPickerView`) groups every team by its `league` string, so a national side is pickable exactly when it is a team record in a league group.
- A country is one team per sport and gender. Soccer men's sides merge across competitions by ESPN id or FIFA code (`Library.catalogNationalTeam`, league string `National teams`); the other sports use one id per country (`fibam-<code>`, `iihf-<code>`, `ehfn-<code>`, new: `fibaw-`, `iihfw-`, `ehfnw-`, `wbsc-`, women's soccer `natw-<espn id>`).
- Competitions were not a catalog concept: only the schedule keys (ESPN league paths, `Tools/schedules/<scope>/<key>/`) and a few passport "league" records existed. **New: `Witness/Resources/Catalog/competitions.json`** lists every tournament below with id (the schedule key), aliases, governing body, cadence, last/next edition, schedule feed and `teamIds` (all real catalog teams). The app reads it through `CompetitionCatalog` (`Witness/Model/CompetitionCatalog.swift`): competition by id/ESPN path/alias, a team's competitions, the `national` flag (generated: `build_tournaments.py` sets it, and adds the three ESPN-only men's soccer national leagues, friendlies and the two qualifying leagues). National-team merging, album titles, the schedule routing of pack-only competitions (`LocalSchedule`, keyed by the competition id) and the team page's "Plays" list all use it; tests in `CompetitionCatalogTests`, `TournamentCatalogTests`, and `Tools/teams/validate_catalog.py`.
- Schedules: the live lookup is ESPN only (`GameLookup`), plus downloaded packs (`LocalSchedule`). FIBA, Sofascore (IIHF, handball) and HockeyTech sourced schedule files are **held back** from published packs (`HELD_SOURCES` in `Tools/packs/build_packs.py`) because their terms are not cleared. So for FIBA, IIHF and IHF competitions the answer is "teams yes, schedule feed no" until that changes (needed: FIBA data licence or API-Basketball for basketball; IIHF/EHF/IHF permission or a paid API for hockey and handball; or ESPN adding them).

## Basketball, as it is now

- National teams: men: Europe 42, Asia/Oceania 34, Americas 32, Africa 24; each country once per gender (`fibam-bih` "Bosnia-Herzegovina", `fibaw-bih`; never "Bosnia and Herzegovina"). Existing ids, names and colors are untouched (`USA` stays white/navy, "Turkey", "United Kingdom"). New sides use the flag's colors and the soccer catalog's country names (Czechia, Congo DR, Ivory Coast).
- Clubs: the 20 EuroLeague clubs 2026-27 (verified against the 2026-27 EuroLeague page: same 20 as before), EuroCup 32, BCL 54, Europe Cup, BAL 2026 (11 of 12: APR withdrew), Intercontinental Cup 2026 (Boca Juniors, Rytas, RSSB Tigers, Beijing Royal Fighters, Shanghai Sharks, NBA G League United). Clubs the catalog already had stay where they are (ACB, BBL...) and are only added to the competition's `teamIds`; new ones sit in their domestic league group (`Liga ACB`, `Lega Basket Serie A`, ...) or, where the catalog has no domestic league, under the continental competition.
- Planned, not a competition yet: NBA Europe (16 teams from 2027, the 2026-27 BCL finalists get places).
- 3x3 basketball (World Cup, Europe Cup, Olympics) is not modelled: the app's `Sport` has no 3x3.

## Gaps, and what would close them

1. **Schedule feeds** (see above): FIBA national and club competitions, IIHF, IHF and EHF have teams but no publishable schedule. ESPN does carry `basketball/fiba` (only the 2026 Women's World Cup today; the men's 2027 World Cup is not listed yet), the Olympic basketball/hockey/baseball leagues, the World Baseball Classic and `hockey/hockey-world-cup`.
2. **Wiring in Swift (done in the follow-up: see the catalog bullet above; what follows is the state before it)** (out of scope: `Witness/Services` and UI were off limits): `MatchupAlbums.leagues(for:)` and `GameLookup.nationalCompetitions` know only soccer's national competitions and `team.league == "National teams"`; basketball/hockey/handball national sides get none of the alias handling ("Bosnia v Qatar" albums for basketball would only find the team by name, not search the FIBA competitions). A team record has one `espnLeague`, so a country's ESPN id is indexed under one competition only (`Library.rebuildTeamIndex`); women's soccer sides share ids across the Women's World Cup, Euro and friendlies and would need the same national-id merge the men's sides have. `Library.nationalLeague` is the single string "National teams". A reader for `competitions.json` (picker sections, album matcher, the lookup's competition list per venue) is the follow-up.
3. **Clubs**: 2 CHL clubs (KooKoo, Graz 99ers), 2 Europa League clubs (Jagiellonia, Torreense), Red Star Belgrade (Conference League) and 3 Club World Cup clubs (Auckland City, Ulsan, Wydad) could not be matched to a catalog team with certainty and are listed as `unmatchedClubs` in `competitions.json`, not added. The men's EHF Champions League and European League 2026-27 fields, women's club competitions (EuroLeague Women, EHF CL Women, Europa Cup), BCL Americas clubs, Caribbean Series and the NBL/Spengler Cup were not extended.
4. **Colors and arenas of new basketball clubs**: colors are the Wikipedia infobox colors, 8 clubs without any (Rilski Sportist, Fribourg Olympic, Opava, Patrioti Levice, Batumi, ASC Ville de Dakar, FUS Rabat, Maktown Flyers) have a neutral color; 15 clubs have no `homeVenueId` because no exact coordinates were found (Derthona, Slavia Prague, Absheron Lions, Batumi, Iraklis, six BAL clubs and others). Soccer clubs from ESPN have no home ground. National teams have no home venue by design (soccer sides never did).
5. **American football internationals**: no tournament exists to list (IFAF World Championship dormant since 2015; flag football at LA 2028 has no field yet). The NFL's international grounds were already in the catalog with `football/nfl`; Estadio Azteca lacked it and now has it.
6. **Rows from ESPN with country unknown**: women's soccer sides and continental clubs have no `countryCode` (ESPN's team list does not give one).

## Not app sports

Rugby (Rugby World Cup, Six Nations, Rugby Championship, Women's Six Nations), cricket (ICC Men's and Women's World Cups, T20 World Cup, Asia Cup), tennis (Davis Cup, Billie Jean King Cup, United Cup) and volleyball (FIVB Worlds, Nations League, VNL) are not in `Sport`, so no team or competition was added; `Tools/teams` already carries 69 rugby and 373 volleyball club records that `build_catalog.py` and `build_packs.py` skip ("the app has no such sport yet"), and `Tools/schedules/sched-tournaments-other` holds rugby World Cup, Six Nations and Olympic sevens schedules. Adding a sport needs a `Sport` case, a picker/passport decision and a tournament pass like this one.

## Regenerating

```
python3 Tools/teams/tournaments/fetch_espn.py          # optional: refresh ESPN's soccer team lists (espn-soccer.json)
python3 Tools/teams/tournaments/build_tournaments.py   # tournaments-teams/leagues/missing-venues.json + Catalog/competitions.json
python3 Tools/teams/validate.py tournaments            # research-format check, 0 errors
python3 Tools/teams/build_catalog.py                   # teams-more.json, venues.json (idempotent)
python3 Tools/teams/validate_catalog.py                # bundled catalog: competitions, no duplicate countries, basketball completeness
python3 Tools/packs/build_packs.py --out dist/packs    # picks up tournaments-* automatically
```
Edit the tables, not the output: `Tools/teams/tournaments/countries.py` (country names, flag colors, FIBA zone), `sets.py` (who plays what, with sources), `clubs-basketball.json` (new clubs with Wikipedia colors and arena coordinates), `competitions.py` (competitions, editions, feeds, venues). The 64 national teams that shipped before live in `legacy-nationals.json` (moved out of `other-sports-teams.json`, values unchanged). `build_catalog.py` now keeps women's sides that share a code with the men's (`gender: "women"`), and `validate.py` accepts national teams and tournament clubs without a home venue and counts leagues from every `*-leagues.json`.
